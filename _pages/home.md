<p class="home-welcome-title">Welcome to the Multiscale Computational Plasma Lab</p>

<p class="home-lead">The <strong>Multiscale Computational Plasma Lab</strong> at UCLA investigates how plasma chemistry and plasma&ndash;material interactions shape the dynamics of low-temperature plasmas. We combine kinetic theory, multiscale simulations, and data-driven inference to develop predictive models that connect microscopic physics to the macroscopic behavior of complex plasma systems.</p>
